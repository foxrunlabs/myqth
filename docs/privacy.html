MYQTH / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

MyQTH is developed by Ryan Clarke. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Location information

MyQTH requests access to your device’s location while you use the app to display your position and calculate geographic coordinates, including Maidenhead grid locators.

Coordinate calculations take place on your device. MyQTH does not send your location to the developer.

You can grant or revoke location permission in your device’s Settings. Without permission, MyQTH cannot obtain an updated position.

Information stored on your device

MyQTH stores the following locally:

  • Your callsign, if you enter one.
  • Preferences such as map zoom, grid visibility, and automatic position updates.
  • Your last known latitude, longitude, horizontal accuracy, and location timestamp.

The saved location allows the app to restore your last known position when reopened. It is replaced when a new location is received. MyQTH does not maintain a location history.

Revoking location permission stops new location access but does not erase the previously saved position. You can clear your callsign in the app’s settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app may retain it. Locally stored information may also be included in device backups managed by your operating system.

Apple Maps

MyQTH uses Apple’s MapKit framework to display maps. Map functionality may communicate with Apple to request map content. Apple handles information associated with its services under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Tracking, advertising, and analytics

MyQTH does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking technologies. It does not require an account. The developer does not collect or sell your callsign or location through the app.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request and provide support.

For privacy questions, contact labs@thefoxrun.net.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app or its information practices. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
